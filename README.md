I'm a Project Manager in the Greater Toronto Area, Ontario, Canada, with 8 years of project management experience across **commercial construction, architectural millwork, interior fit-outs and healthcare-related projects**. I hold the **Project Management Professional (PMP®)** certification.

I also run **[Project Manager Land](https://www.youtube.com/@ProjectManagerLand)**, a YouTube channel that makes project management easy to understand, practical and useful in the real world. It's aimed at beginners, project coordinators and PMP candidates.

> "Project management isn't about having a perfect plan. It's about keeping people connected and helping them decide what to do next when things change."
